Personal Mail Assistant privacy policy

Last updated 06/10/2026.

Personal Mail Assistant is used by one person, the owner of this GitHub account, with the owner's own Gmail account. It is not offered or distributed to anyone else.

What it can access

With the owner's permission, Google lets it view, compose, send and change messages in that one Gmail account, without permanently deleting any, and change the account's mail settings and filters. These are Google's gmail.modify and gmail.settings.basic permissions.

Where the data goes

  • The sign-in token is stored only on the owner's computer.
  • Messages are read on that computer. When the owner asks the assistant to work with their mail, the parts it reads are sent to Anthropic as part of that conversation. Anthropic handles that data under its own privacy policy.
  • The tool saves a message or attachment to disk only when the owner asks it to.
  • Mail is not sold, used for advertising, or shared with anyone other than Anthropic as described above.

Removing access

The owner can remove the tool's access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. After that, the token left on the owner's computer no longer works.
